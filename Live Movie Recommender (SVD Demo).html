            How a prediction is made

            Every score on this page is the same sum of four parts. The first three are averages; the fourth is the linear algebra.

            r̂ui = μ + bu + bi + pu · vi

            1. Build the rating matrix

            2. Remove the averages

            μ is the mean rating. bu says how generously a user rates and bi how well liked a film is, both shrunk by λ = 10 when there are few ratings. Subtracting them leaves the residual matrix Z.

            3. Factorise the residuals with the SVD

            Z = U Σ VT  ≈  U12 Σ12 V12T

            Keeping the 12 largest singular values (chosen on a validation set) gives the closest rank-12 matrix to Z. Each user becomes a vector pu (a row of U12Σ12) and each film a vector vi (a row of V12).

            4. A new viewer: fold in

            When you rate films on the first tab, your residuals zj = rj − μ − bu − bj are projected onto the same factors: p = Σ zj vj, which equals z V12. No retraining is needed.
